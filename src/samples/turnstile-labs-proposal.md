# Turnstile Labs - Proposal for Harbor City Arena Ticketing

FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Turnstile Labs is an invented company.

## 1. Executive Summary

Turnstile Labs proposes its Turnstile One platform for primary ticketing, season ticket management, resale and mobile entry at Harbor City Arena. Turnstile One currently powers ticketing for 60 venues across North America.

## 2. Resale Marketplace

Turnstile One includes Turnstile Exchange, a fully integrated resale marketplace. Fans can list tickets for resale directly from their account. Venues set resale price floors and price caps per event or per section, and resold tickets are reissued to the buyer with a new barcode.

## 3. Mobile Entry and Scanning

All tickets are delivered to the Turnstile mobile app or to Apple and Google wallets with rotating barcodes that refresh every 15 seconds. Our handheld scanners and turnstile integrations cache the full event manifest locally, so scanning continues for up to 12 hours without internet connectivity and syncs automatically when the connection returns.

## 4. Performance and Scale

Turnstile One has processed 42,000 ticket purchases in the first 10 minutes of a single on-sale. Every high-demand on-sale is protected by Turnstile Queue, our virtual waiting room, which admits fans in randomized order.

## 5. Security and Compliance

Turnstile Labs is certified as a PCI DSS Level 1 Service Provider. Our current Attestation of Compliance, dated March 2026, is included as Appendix B. We also complete an annual SOC 2 Type II audit.

## 6. Support

Event-day support is available 24/7 from our Network Operations Center. For Severity 1 issues, we guarantee a response within 10 minutes. A named account manager is assigned to the arena.

## 7. Accessibility

The Turnstile One fan purchase flow has been independently audited and conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Box office staff can hold, release and sell accessible seating and companion seats through a dedicated ADA inventory tool.

## 8. Integrations

Turnstile One includes a native Salesforce connector that syncs fan profiles and purchases nightly. We have certified integrations with major access-control turnstile manufacturers; compatibility with the arena's specific turnstile model will be confirmed during discovery.

## 9. Pricing

Fan service fee: $4.25 per ticket.
Venue platform license: $180,000 per year.
Payment processing: 2.6% of gross ticket revenue, charged to the venue.
Resale: buyers pay a 10% fee and sellers pay a 5% fee on Turnstile Exchange.

## 10. Implementation

Our standard implementation plan for a venue of this size takes 75 days from contract signature to the first live on-sale.
