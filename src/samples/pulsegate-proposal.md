# PulseGate Ticketing - Response to Harbor City Arena RFP

FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. PulseGate Ticketing is an invented company.

## 1. About PulseGate

PulseGate Ticketing serves 35 arenas and stadiums. Our platform, PulseGate Live, covers primary sales, season memberships, resale and entry.

## 2. Ticket Resale

PulseGate Live includes an integrated resale marketplace inside the fan account. Venues can set a minimum resale price for each event. Resale price caps are on our product roadmap for the second half of 2027.

## 3. Mobile Tickets

Tickets are delivered as mobile tickets with dynamic barcodes that rotate every 30 seconds. If the venue loses internet connectivity, our scanners switch to offline mode and continue validating tickets against a locally stored list.

## 4. High-Demand On-Sales

PulseGate Live runs on elastic cloud infrastructure that scales automatically for high-demand on-sales. Fans enter a virtual waiting room before purchase when demand is high.

## 5. Payment Security

PulseGate is a PCI DSS Level 1 compliant service provider. Our Attestation of Compliance is available on request under NDA.

## 6. Your Data

Harbor City Arena will own all fan and transaction data collected through PulseGate Live. The venue may export all data at any time through self-service exports and the reporting API, and we will deliver a complete data export within 30 days of contract termination.

## 7. Accessibility

Our purchase flow conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Accessible and companion seating can be managed as a separate inventory class with its own pricing and holds.

## 8. Integrations

PulseGate Live offers a bidirectional Salesforce integration for fan profiles, purchases and attendance. Our entry system is compatible with turnstiles from most major manufacturers.

## 9. Fees

Fan service fee: $3.10 per ticket.
Venue fees: no annual license fee. The venue pays a 2.9% payment processing fee on gross ticket revenue.

## 10. Timeline

We expect Harbor City Arena to be live in approximately 120 days from contract signature.
