# Harbor City Arena - Ticketing Platform Requirements

FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Harbor City Arena, the Harbor City Gulls and every vendor in this demo are invented.

## 1. Background

Harbor City Arena is an 18,500-seat multipurpose venue hosting approximately 140 events per year, including 44 home games for the Harbor City Gulls. Our current ticketing contract ends on June 30, 2027. We are seeking a replacement primary ticketing platform.

## 2. Scope

The selected platform will handle primary ticket sales, season ticket management, resale, mobile entry and reporting for all events at the arena.

## 3. Requirements

R1. Integrated resale: The platform must include an integrated secondary (resale) marketplace that lets fans resell tickets and lets the venue set price floors and price caps.
R2. Mobile entry: Tickets must be delivered as mobile tickets with rotating (dynamic) barcodes, and gate scanning must keep working if the venue loses internet connectivity.
R3. On-sale capacity: The platform must handle at least 15,000 ticket purchases within the first 10 minutes of a high-demand on-sale, with a virtual waiting room.
R4. Payment security: The vendor must be PCI DSS Level 1 compliant and provide its current Attestation of Compliance.
R5. Data ownership: The venue must own all fan and transaction data, with a full export available at any time and upon contract termination.
R6. Support SLA: The vendor must provide 24/7 support on event days with a guaranteed response time of 15 minutes or less for critical (Severity 1) issues.
R7. Accessibility: The fan purchase flow must conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and the platform must support management of accessible (ADA) seating inventory.
R8. Fee transparency: The proposal must state all fees charged to fans and to the venue, including per-ticket service fees and any platform, license or processing fees.
R9. Integrations: The platform must integrate with our CRM (Salesforce) and with our existing access-control turnstiles.
R10. Implementation timeline: The platform must be fully live within 90 days of contract signature.

## 4. Submission

Proposals are due by December 1, 2026. Harbor City Arena staff will make the final selection.
