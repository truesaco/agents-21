# Bleacher Box - Ticketing Proposal for Harbor City Arena

FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Bleacher Box is an invented company.

## 1. Overview

Bleacher Box is a fast-growing ticketing company built for mid-size venues. We keep things simple and affordable.

## 2. Features

- Primary ticketing and season tickets
- Fan-to-fan resale with venue-controlled price floors and caps
- Mobile tickets with rotating QR codes
- Real-time sales dashboards

## 3. Gate Entry

Our scanning app runs on standard smartphones. An internet connection is required for ticket validation at the gate.

## 4. Scalability

Bleacher Box has handled on-sales of up to 8,000 tickets in the first hour. A virtual queue can be enabled for any event.

## 5. Security

All payments are processed with industry-standard encryption through our payment partner.

## 6. Data

The venue owns its fan and transaction data. Data can be exported as CSV files at any time from the venue dashboard.

## 7. Support

Our support team is available 24/7 by phone and chat on event days. Critical issues receive a response within 15 minutes, guaranteed in our service level agreement.

## 8. Integrations

Bleacher Box offers an open REST API. A Salesforce integration can be built using the API.

## 9. Pricing

Fan service fee: $2.50 per ticket. No license fees for the venue.

## 10. Onboarding

Most venues are live within 45 days of signing.
